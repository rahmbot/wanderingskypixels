Launch list is a snapshot from Launch Library 2 by The Space Devs, the data behind Space Launch Schedule; dates marked TBD are placeholders, so set the real liftoff above once it's announced. No guessing: a launch unlocks only when its direction is confirmed from published launch info, or is a repeat of a mission family we've already documented (like Space Station flights), and its launch time is firm. Everything else shows as pending. Flight profile is a typical first 10 minutes of a Falcon-class launch. Verdict thresholds: under 2.5° up is No, under 6° is Unlikely, 6–10° is Maybe. The sunlit check uses the real Sun position. A clear, flat horizon is assumed. Weather is not checked; use the forecast link.
