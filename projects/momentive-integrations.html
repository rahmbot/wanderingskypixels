Design Rationale
The Pillar 2 Story

Plug-and-play, not custom work

This prototype demonstrates what "productized connectors" actually means as a user experience. A nonprofit admin — not a developer — can connect two Momentive products in three steps. No API keys. No custom integration project. The platform handles the data mapping, the SSO handoff, and the ongoing sync automatically.

The AI Angle

MomentiveIQ as the config layer

The AI-assisted setup step shows how MomentiveIQ can reduce configuration friction to near-zero. A user describes their workflow in plain English — the AI maps the fields, sets the trigger, and explains what it did. This is the kind of AI that drives product adoption, not just conversation.

The Business Case

1 product → 2 products, felt on day one

The moment a GiveSmart customer connects Event Management, they've moved from a single-product user to a multi-product user. The integration is the nudge. By making it frictionless and free, the platform removes every barrier between "I use GiveSmart" and "I use the Momentive suite."

What's Next

Phase 2: The partner SDK

Once the native connection model is proven, the same integration kit opens to external partners — Salesforce, Blackbaud, university portals, hospital CRMs. Each new connector is a sellable product in a catalog, not a one-off project. The platform becomes a marketplace.
